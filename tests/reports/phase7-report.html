One trade-off to know about. On Development, today's engine lands minutes closer to equal (13 against 29 on the distance score), but it gets there with about 40 subs at 22 stoppages a match and 111 broken guarantees across the squads, mostly 2 to 6 changes at once and very short spells. The new planner keeps the minutes range as tight (6.6' against 7.0') with about 13 subs, because guarantees come before minutes in the order you agreed. On Competitive and Performance the range is wider on purpose: targets follow level.

What a coach gets now

  • Live re-planning. The past stays exactly as played; only the rest is re-planned, starting from the plan the coach has seen. Help level is a team setting (off, big problems only, keep the next change, full re-plan). "Go strongest now" re-plans from that minute.
  • Mark available again. A player who was out rejoins the plan; their time out is never counted as minutes owed.
  • Team approach. Four approach cards with a live preview from the squad, five fine-tune sliders, and Advanced settings. Every value says where it came from.
  • Today and player exceptions on each fixture: late, early, a minutes limit, extra minutes, must start, bench start, keeper today.
  • Scorecard strip and "Why?" on every change and every player. For example: "If Noah came off instead: Leo sits out 22', over the 19' maximum."
  • Onboarding. Team, formats and squad, then 15 to 18 plain questions over 5 pages, a setup review where each setting shows "Set for you" with the reason, and a tour that calls out what was set.

Choices I made that you may want to change

  • A team set up through onboarding plans with the new planner straight away (the review page has a box to turn it off). Existing teams stay on today's engine until they switch it on in Squad › Team approach. Making it the default for everyone is phase 8.
  • When a coach makes their own change while a planned change is waiting for Done, any waiting change that touches the same players or positions is treated as skipped. Waiting changes elsewhere stay.
  • If no keeper is left, the player the coach puts in goal counts as today's keeper and stays in goal for the rest of the match, including the next period.
  • The questionnaire asks for an answer to every question rather than pre-selecting one; "Skip" gives Balanced.
  • "When can you make changes?" is asked once per team, even if the team plays more than one format.
  • Pairs (together or apart) are a light preference, below positions.
