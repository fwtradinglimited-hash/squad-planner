Scorecard Baseline

The new scorecard marks any plan against your rules, tier by tier, and says why. Here it marks three plans for your midfield squad and every plan today's engine makes for 14 test squads. Nothing in the app has changed yet.

What I need from you: check that the scorecard ranks plans the way you would. If it does, I build the new planner on top of it. If it doesn't, we fix the scorecard first, because the planner will chase whatever the scorecard rewards.

How a plan is judged

A higher tier always wins. Each tier leaves a little room so the tiers below can still improve the plan. Numbers shown are the proposed Balanced ethos for a 50-minute 7v7 match; other ethos settings move the numbers, never the order.

0

Rules that can't be broken

One player per position, the right numbers, no "Do not use" positions, only keepers in goal, nobody playing when they aren't there, caps and league minimums.

1

Guarantees

Broken as little as possible, and every break is listed.

Minute floor 68% of an equal share · no all-Developing unit · rest at most 19' · spells at least 8' · at most 2 changes at once during play (period breaks are free)

2

Minutes

Each player's minutes against their target. Inside the band costs nothing; outside it, the cost grows quickly. Keeper time doesn't count, so keepers are owed outfield time too.

Band ± 4' around each target

3

Strength

Level × position fit for everyone on the pitch. Balanced lifts the weakest spell towards the average, with some weight on the first and last minutes. Shown per player on the 0–10 level scale.

4

Positions

How well players fit where they play (Preferred 1.0, Comfortable 0.85, Can play 0.6), plus variety and "plays well with" or "keep apart" pairs when set.

5

Smoothness

Fewer stoppages, fewer subs and position moves, and keeping the plan the coach has already seen.

Questions for you

  1. The order of the tiers. Rules, then guarantees, then minutes, then strength, then positions, then smoothness. Does that match how you'd judge a plan from the touchline?
  2. The midfield ranking. The staggered plan wins under every ethos, and today's engine comes last under three of the four. Is that your judgement too?
  3. The Balanced numbers. A ± 4' band, a floor of 68% of an equal share, no rest longer than 19', no spell shorter than 8', and at most 2 changes at once during play. Too strict, too loose, or about right?
  4. Changes at period breaks. I've made changes at half-time (and quarter breaks) free of the "at once" limit, because play has stopped anyway. Agree?
  5. Keepers. With keeper time not counting, the scorecard expects a keeper who kept goal for a half to get a full outfield share in the other half as well. Is that what you want?
