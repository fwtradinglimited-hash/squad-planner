What it does

  • Plans the team for youTell it who's available and how you like to play. It picks the line-up and a substitution plan, keepers included, so minutes even out.
  • Runs match day from your phoneMatch clock, sub alerts, goals and scorers. Someone's late or injured? Change it on the touchline and it re-plans the rest of the game.
  • Keeps the season in one placeMinutes, positions, goals and awards for every player, with a check that flags anyone falling behind.
  • Leaves you in chargeEvery suggestion comes with a reason, nothing changes without you seeing it, and you can pick the team yourself instead.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, it's free during the beta. If that ever changes, beta coaches will hear about it well in advance.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Co-Coach runs in your phone's browser. Add it to your home screen and it opens like any other app, even with no signal.

What about my players' details?

You only enter first names or nicknames. Your squad and matches are saved on your phone, not on our servers, and nothing about your players is shared.

Will it work for my team?

It handles any squad size, 3-a-side to 11-a-side, any match length and any number of periods, with or without a goalkeeper.
