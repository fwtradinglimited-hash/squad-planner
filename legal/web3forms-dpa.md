## Main terms (as read on 14 October 2026)

- Web3Creative processes data only on our documented instructions and as
  needed to provide the service (form submissions, email delivery, file storage).
- Staff and contractors with access are bound by confidentiality.
- Security: encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access, web
  application firewall, rate limiting and backups.
- Sub-processors: Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare, Hetzner Online,
  CleanTalk, Automattic (Akismet), SparrowDesk and Microsoft (Clarity).
  At least 14 days' notice of changes, and we may object. Paddle (payments)
  is an independent controller and isn't a sub-processor.
- Breaches: they tell us within 72 hours of becoming aware.
- Retention: submissions are kept for up to three years; after we stop
  using the service, deleted within 90 days (subject to backup cycles).
- Audits: information on request, or one audit a year with 30 days' notice.
- International transfers: the EU standard contractual clauses, with the
  UK International Data Transfer Addendum where applicable.
- The agreement takes precedence over Web3Forms' general terms on personal
  data, and the standard contractual clauses take precedence over the agreement.
