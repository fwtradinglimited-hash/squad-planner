Privacy Policy

Squad & Rotation Planner (the "app") · Version 2026-10-09 · Last updated 9 October 2026

The short version
  • The app is for adult coaches and team managers (18+). It is not for children to use.
  • Today, your teams, players and matches are stored only on your own device. We can't see them.
  • We never ask for surnames, photos, dates of birth, contact details for children, locations, health information or anything else about players beyond what is needed to plan and record matches: a first name or nickname, optional shirt number, preferred positions and match results/minutes.
  • If you send feedback, or the app hits an error, it sends us a technical log and the match you are working on, with player, team and opposition names replaced by "Player 1, 2...".
  • No advertising, no tracking or analytics, no selling of data.

If we later store that data for you in the cloud, we will act as your processor and will update this policy first (section 9).

2. Children's information

Coaches record information about children (their players). We take that seriously, so the app is built to collect as little as possible:

  • First names or nicknames only. The app reminds you not to enter surnames and asks you to confirm before saving what looks like a full name. Use an initial if two players share a name.
  • No photos, dates of birth, addresses, school, contact details, medical or behavioural information are requested or needed.
  • Children don't use the app. It is designed for and aimed at adults. If we learn that a child has been using it, we will delete any information we hold about them.
  • Coaches should only enter players' first names if they have the permission of the club and the players' parents or carers, in line with the club's own privacy arrangements. The app asks you to confirm this when you set up a team.
  • Free-text match notes are yours alone. Please keep them to football matters, and don't record sensitive personal information about any child in them. Notes are never included in diagnostic reports (section 3D).

B. Things you create and share yourself

Line-up and summary images, and backup files, are made on your device and shared only if and where you choose (for example in a parents' group chat). They include players' first names, so share them only with people who should see them.

C. Feedback you send us

If you use "Send feedback / report a problem", we receive: your rating and comment, your name and email only if you choose to give them, and the technical information described below. We use it to understand and fix problems and improve the app (our legitimate interests; if you ask for a reply, to respond to you).

D. Technical diagnostics (feedback and errors)

The app keeps a short, rolling log on your device of errors, the screens you visit, buttons you tap, settings you change and a summary of what the planner produced. It never records what you type. This log is sent to us:

  • every time you send feedback or report a problem; and
  • automatically when the app logs an error, so that we can fix it.

Each report also includes the match you are working on (or your most recent one): format, line-up, rotation settings, planned and actual substitutions, goals and assists and awards. Player, team, opposition and venue names are replaced with neutral labels ("Player 1", "Team", "Opposition") before sending, and your match notes are removed. The report also contains app version, device and browser type, screen size, whether the app is installed, approximate counts (players, matches), a random install code (not linked to your identity), and your first name if you gave one. The information is used only to find and fix faults and to check the planner works fairly (our legitimate interests in keeping the app working properly).

Your own typed comment in a feedback form is not altered, so please don't put children's full names or personal details in it.

E. Information we do not collect

No advertising or marketing identifiers, no analytics or tracking tools, no location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos, no payment information (the app is currently free), and no information from or about children beyond what a coach chooses to enter as described above.

4. Who receives feedback and diagnostics

Reports are delivered using Web3Forms (operated by Web3Creative, based in India), which acts as our processor. It stores submissions on its servers for up to three years, then deletes them, and emails them to us. Its infrastructure providers include Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare and Hetzner (EU), and it uses spam filtering services that see the sender's IP address. Web3Forms processes data under a data processing agreement with international-transfer safeguards (EU standard contractual clauses and the UK Addendum). Your IP address is visible to these services when a report is sent. We do not share reports with anyone else, and we do not sell data.

5. How long we keep information

  • On your device: until you delete it (team menu → Privacy & data → Delete all my data, or deleting a team), clear your browser data or uninstall the app.
  • Feedback and diagnostic reports we hold: we aim to delete them within 12 months of receipt, or sooner on request. (Web3Forms' own copy is removed after up to three years, or earlier when we delete it.)

6. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to give you a copy of, correct, delete, restrict or stop using information we hold about you, and to move it to another service. To exercise these rights over information we hold, email fw.trading.limited@gmail.com. We'll normally reply within one month. Because reports use anonymised labels and a random install code, we may ask you for the install code (shown in the Privacy & data screen) to find yours.

Parents and carers: your child's first name and match record are held by their coach or club, on the coach's device. To see, correct or remove them, please ask the coach or club. If you can't resolve it, contact us and we will help where we can.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, 0303 123 1113) if you are unhappy with how your information is handled, though we would like the chance to put things right first.

7. Security

Data in the app stays on your device, protected by your device's own security (screen lock, encryption). Reports are sent over encrypted connections. Please keep your device locked, treat backup files and exported images as you would any list of children's names, and don't share them more widely than necessary. We cannot recover local data for you.

8. International transfers

Feedback and diagnostic reports are processed by Web3Forms and its providers in several countries, including India, under the safeguards in section 4. We will tell you before any change that moves squad or match data outside the UK or EEA.

9. Cloud features in future

We plan to add optional accounts, backup and sharing. Nothing in this section applies today. Before any squad or match data is stored in the cloud we will update this policy and ask you to review it. Our commitments:

  • It will stay optional, with the app still usable offline on your device.
  • Squad and match data will be stored in the UK or EU, encrypted in transit and at rest, and visible only to the coach and anyone they invite to the team.
  • We will hold it as your processor, using it only to provide the service, never for advertising or sale, and will offer a data processing agreement for clubs.
  • You will be able to export and permanently delete your account and all of its data from within the app.
  • We will keep collecting only first names, positions and match data, with no photos or surnames, and no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • For accounts we will hold your email address and your team name(s) to run the account (performance of our contract with you).
